一、概述
1. 产品特性
（1）G12 系列采用高通 6nm 处理器，搭载安卓 13（64 位）嵌入式系统，操作流畅，
生态开放，可安装市面上绝大多数 APP。采用全新高功率、高带宽的 2.4/5.8G 双频无
线模块，支持自动跳频和 MCS 自主动态调整，图数传距离可达 20KM。
（2）G12 支持 SIM 卡网口、USB 接口、type-c 接口、ppm 口等丰富接口提供 SDK
开发包，支持视频悬浮，支持主流飞控的地面站。
（3）1920*1080 分辨率、5.5 寸高亮显示屏，超大的屏占比，在阳光环境下依然可以
清晰地查看实时显示信息。
（4）采用高能量密度的锂离子电池，满电可工作 4～8 个小时，一贯的荒野求生思维
让您对设备的可靠性毫无牵挂。
2. 主要用途及适用范围
G12 适用于对直升机、固定翼、多旋翼、车、船等无人设备的视频图像传输（需
选配摄像头）、数据传输和操控。
3. 使用环境条件
注意：
a）环境温度：-10°C~55°C。
b）贮存温度：-20°C ~60°C。
c）相对湿度：Not exceed 85%。
d）大气压力：86kPa～106kPa。
e）使用地点不允许有爆炸危险的介质，周围介质中不应含有腐蚀金属和破坏绝缘的气

9. 遥控器对频
接收机 1：GR01
方式一：
①遥控器开机、接收机通电，通电后绿灯常亮，蓝灯间歇闪烁，风扇间歇转动。
②长按接收机对频按钮，直到蓝灯快速闪烁后松开。

1. Product Features
(1) The G12 series adopts a Qualcomm 6nm processor and is equipped with an
Android 13 (64-bit) embedded system, ensuring smooth operation and an
open ecosystem that supports the installation of most applications available
on the market. It uses an all-new high-power, high-bandwidth 2.4/5.8G

autonomous dynamic adjustment, with an image and data transmission range
of up to 20km.
(2) The G12 is equipped with a variety of interfaces including SIM card Ethernet
port, USB port, Type-C port, and PPM port. It provides an SDK development kit,
supports video floating window, and is compatible with ground stations of
mainstream flight controllers.
(3) It features a 5.5-inch high-brightness display with a 1920*1080 resolution
and an ultra-large screen-to-body ratio, allowing clear viewing of real-time
displayed information even in direct sunlight.
(4) Powered by a high-energy-density lithium-ion battery, it can work for 4 to 8
hours on a full charge. The consistent "wilderness survival" design philosophy
ensures you have no worries about the device's reliability.
2. Main Uses and Application Scope
The G12 is suitable for video image transmission (a camera is required as an
optional accessory), data transmission, and control of unmanned devices such
as helicopters, fixed-wing aircraft, multi-rotor drones, unmanned vehicles, and
unmanned boats.

9. Remote Controller Frequency Pairing
Receiver 1: GR01
Method 1:
① Power on the remote controller and power up the receiver. After power-up,
the green light stays on, the blue light flashes intermittently, and the fan runs
intermittently.
② Press and hold the receiver's pairing button until the blue light flashes
rapidly, then release it.
③ Open the Device Assistant on the remote controller, tap Pairing → Start
Pairing, confirm the pairing, and wait for the "Pairing Successful" prompt.
④ Upon successful pairing, the receiver's indicator light will stay solid blue.
Method 2:
① Power the receiver on and off quickly four times, then power it on a fifth
time to activate the pairing mode. The blue light will flash rapidly.
② Open the Device Assistant on the remote controller, tap Pairing → Start
Pairing, confirm the pairing, and wait for the "Pairing Successful" prompt.
③ Upon successful pairing, the receiver's indicator light will stay solid blue.
Note: Keep the remote controller and receiver within 50 cm during pairing.
Receiver 2: G12 RX
Method 1:
① Power on the remote controller and power up the receiver. After power-up,
the red light stays on and the green light flashes intermittently.
② Short-circuit the GND and KEY pins of the receiver until the green light
flashes rapidly, then release.
③ Open the Device Assistant on the remote controller, tap Pairing → Start
Pairing, confirm the pairing, and wait for the "Pairing Successful" prompt.
④ Upon successful pairing, the receiver's indicator light will stay solid green.

Do not immerse the remote controller in water. If it gets wet, wipe it
immediately with a soft, dry cloth and turn off the power right away.

do not drop the battery.
